# CLAUDE_LANDING_PAGE_STRUCTURE.md --- UBUNTUSPEAKS Strategic Facilitation Page Structure

## Purpose

This file defines the **exact structure of the Strategic Facilitation
landing page** for UBUNTUSPEAKS.

The page is designed to convert **corporate leaders and executive
decision makers** into consultation calls.

The structure follows conversion patterns commonly used by consulting
and advisory firms.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Page Structure Overview

1.  Hero Section
2.  The Organizational Problem
3.  Lead Capture
4.  Authority & Expertise
5.  Facilitation Services
6.  Organizational Outcomes
7.  Ideal Clients
8.  Secondary Lead Capture
9.  Final Call to Action

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Hero Section

The hero section must immediately communicate:

• Strategic facilitation\
• Executive audience\
• Leadership alignment outcomes

### Required Elements

Headline\
Subheadline\
Primary CTA button

### Example CTA

Schedule a Leadership Consultation

### Mobile Requirements

• CTA visible above the fold\
• Headline readable on phone screens\
• Buttons large enough for thumb tapping

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. The Organizational Problem

This section explains the leadership challenges organizations face when
difficult conversations lack structure.

### Common Problems

• Leadership misalignment\
• Circular meetings without decisions\
• Avoided difficult conversations\
• Strategy discussions that stall\
• Communication breakdowns across teams

The reader should quickly recognize their own leadership environment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Lead Capture

Offer a valuable executive resource.

### Recommended Lead Magnet

The Executive Guide to Productive Leadership Conversations

### Form Fields

First Name\
Email Address

### Placement

Place lead capture:

• After the problem section\
• Before the final call-to-action

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Authority & Expertise

This section establishes credibility.

### Highlight

• 20+ years leadership experience\
• Global organizational work\
• Cultural intelligence expertise\
• Psychological safety leadership\
• Positive Intelligence coaching\
• Multilingual facilitation (English, French, Spanish)

This section reassures executive visitors they are engaging a qualified
facilitator.

# 9. Final Call to Action

End with a strong invitation to connect.

### Example Closing Message

### CTA

Schedule a Leadership Consultation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Mobile Optimization Rules

Because most visitors will arrive on phones:

• Use stacked layouts\
• Avoid dense text blocks\
• Ensure CTA buttons are large\
• Maintain clear vertical spacing\
• Ensure forms are easy to complete on mobile

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Design Principle

The page should feel like a **premium consulting firm website**, not a
coaching funnel or SaaS product page.

Tone: executive, strategic, professional.
