# CLAUDE_COPY_GUIDELINES.md --- Executive Copywriting Rules for UBUNTUSPEAKS

## Purpose

This document guides Claude in writing website copy for the
**UBUNTUSPEAKS Strategic Facilitation Landing Page**.

The goal is to produce **executive‑level consulting copy**, not generic
marketing text.

The copy should resonate with:

-   CEOs
-   Managing Partners
-   Executive Leadership Teams
-   HR and Organizational Development Leaders
-   Corporate Strategy Leaders
-   Global Team Leaders

The writing should feel similar in tone to advisory firms such as:

-   McKinsey & Company
-   Boston Consulting Group
-   Deloitte
-   Korn Ferry

The language should communicate **clarity, authority, and strategic
insight**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Voice and Tone

All copy should feel:

-   Strategic
-   Calm
-   Insightful
-   Professional
-   Human-centered
-   Confident but not boastful

Avoid sounding like:

-   a motivational speaker
-   a course creator
-   a startup founder
-   an influencer brand

Executives should feel they are reading **advisory insight**, not
marketing hype.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Writing Style

Preferred style:

-   clear and concise
-   structured sections
-   logical flow
-   short paragraphs
-   precise language

Avoid:

-   buzzwords
-   exaggerated claims
-   overly emotional language
-   filler words

Example:

Bad: "Unlock your leadership potential and transform your organization
today."

Better: "Leadership teams often struggle to navigate complex
conversations without structure. Strategic facilitation helps
organizations move from discussion to decisive action."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Sentence Structure

Use short, clear sentences.

Guidelines:

-   Prefer 12--20 word sentences
-   Break long ideas into multiple sentences
-   Avoid dense text blocks

Executives often skim content.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Paragraph Length

Keep paragraphs short.

Mobile rule:

Maximum **3--4 lines per paragraph on mobile screens**.

This improves readability for phone users.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Headline Writing

Headlines must communicate **strategic value immediately**.

Good examples:

-   Strategic Facilitation for Leadership Teams Navigating Complex
    Decisions
-   When Leadership Conversations Stall, Facilitation Creates Clarity
-   Align Leadership Teams and Move Conversations Toward Decisions

Avoid vague or inspirational headlines.

Bad examples:

-   Lead With Purpose
-   Transform Your Future

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Subheadline Writing

Subheadlines should clarify:

-   what the service does
-   who it helps
-   the outcome it creates

Example:

"Strategic facilitation designed for executive teams facing complex
conversations, misalignment, or critical decisions."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Problem Framing

Strong consulting copy begins by **naming the leadership challenge
clearly**.

Focus on real problems such as:

-   leadership misalignment
-   circular meetings
-   unresolved tension between leaders
-   strategy discussions without decisions
-   cross-team communication breakdowns

The reader should immediately think:

"This describes our leadership meetings."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Authority Signals

Authority should appear naturally through:

-   experience statements
-   expertise references
-   global leadership work
-   facilitation philosophy

Example:

"Drawing on more than two decades of leadership development and
facilitation experience, Hermence helps organizations navigate the
conversations leadership teams often avoid."

Avoid exaggerated claims.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Service Descriptions

Each service description should answer:

1.  What the engagement is
2.  What challenge it solves
3.  What outcome it creates

Example:

**Power Hour Executive Facilitation**

A focused facilitation session designed to help leadership teams work
through urgent decisions or sensitive conversations that require neutral
guidance and structured dialogue.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Outcome Language

Executives care about **results**, not activities.

Focus on outcomes such as:

-   leadership alignment
-   clearer decisions
-   stronger collaboration
-   productive strategy discussions
-   psychologically safe dialogue

Avoid focusing only on process.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Call‑to‑Action Language

CTAs should feel **consultative**, not pushy.

Good examples:

-   Schedule a Leadership Consultation
-   Discuss Your Leadership Challenge
-   Explore Strategic Facilitation Support
-   Book a Confidential Consultation

Avoid aggressive CTAs like:

-   Buy Now
-   Sign Up Today
-   Get Started Immediately

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Email Capture Copy

The email capture section should feel like an **executive resource**,
not a marketing funnel.

Example headline:

"Is Your Leadership Team Fully Aligned?"

Example description:

"Download the Executive Guide to Productive Leadership Conversations and
learn how high‑performing leadership teams navigate complex discussions
and reach clear decisions."

Tone must remain:

-   professional
-   concise
-   insightful

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Mobile Writing Rules

Because many visitors read on phones:

-   keep sentences shorter
-   avoid long paragraphs
-   use clear headings
-   maintain generous spacing
-   use bullet lists where helpful

Mobile readability is essential.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Consistency Rules

Maintain consistent terminology across the page.

Preferred terms:

-   strategic facilitation
-   leadership conversations
-   executive teams
-   organizational alignment

Avoid switching terminology unnecessarily.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Copy Quality Check

Before publishing copy, confirm:

-   tone sounds like an executive advisor
-   sentences are easy to read on mobile
-   the leadership problem is clearly defined
-   services are explained simply
-   outcomes are emphasized
-   CTAs feel professional

If the writing sounds like generic marketing, rewrite it.
